How to trade it

  1. Start on the Home tab. Check the market, take the #1 index and a green sector. Big 3 longs work best when the stock's sector is green and the stock is strong (Top down filter).
  2. Only trade A+ or +12 setups. Squeeze on (blue), trend, structure and momentum all bullish, Big 3 Score 7 or more, price above the trailing stop. If it is not A+, walk away.
  3. Enter near the 21 EMA. Buy inside the buy zone, no more than 1.25 ATR above the 21 EMA. If the Entry column says Extended, wait for a pullback.
  4. Stop below the 21 EMA. A daily close under the 21 EMA ends the trade. The trailing stop is the looser backup.
  5. Target 3 ATR. The bull target is the 21 EMA plus 3 ATR. Take profits there, or trail the rest with the trailing stop.
  6. Watch the labels while in it. Momentum turning red or the histogram fading for several bars is an early warning. Close below the trailing stop means out.
  7. Slingshot is the early version: squeeze on and the stack bullish while the histogram is still below zero but rising. Use smaller size until momentum turns green.
  8. Shorts are the mirror image: A+ Bear, enter near the 21 EMA, stop above it, target 21 EMA minus 3 ATR.

How to trade it

  1. Buyers only for real trades. Your backtests on 1,755 stocks (2008 to 2026) showed buying at -2.5 in a flat channel and holding to the line won 77% of 3,702 trades, while shorting the top lost money (44% win). Use the Sellers list to take profits or avoid buying, not to short.
  2. Flat channels first. The best results came from flat channels (slope under 0.15 deviations per 20 bars). Steep down channels can keep falling, so use the Channel filter.
  3. Wait for a green candle. In your earlier 50 stock tests a confirming close beat buying the first touch (85% vs 77%). Use the Candle filter.
  4. Target the middle line. The regression line is the exit. Winners took a median of about 35 to 40 days to get there.
  5. Use a 20% disaster stop. Tight stops at the next band killed the win rate. No stop kept the win rate at 77% but the worst trade lost 90%; a 20% stop dropped it to 68% and capped the worst near 46% (gaps). The Stop column shows 20% under price.
  6. Beyond -4 is a trend break, not a bigger discount. Those are hidden unless you pick All past 2.5.
  7. Bands are probability zones, not support. About 1 in 5 trades fell a full extra deviation before working. Size small enough to sit through it.

How to trade it

  1. Filter first. Trade when the Daily, Weekly and Monthly banners agree (Banners filter). A neutral monthly means skip the ticker.
  2. Pick the timeframe for the trade. Daily edge for short swings, Weekly for swings, Monthly for position trades, Yearly blue edge for LEAP trades.
  3. Enter at a level, never mid zone. Time the entry on a 15 minute or 1 hour chart at the level the setup names.
  4. Edge reversal (your main setup). Price reaches the lower edge and prints a minor low or inside and up. Stop under the pattern low, target the upper edge.
  5. Edge rejection. Price drops under the lower edge and closes back above it. Stop under the wick low, target the decelerator.
  6. Accelerator hold. Banner bullish, price pulls back to the accelerator (green dots) and holds. Stop on a close under the mid, target the upper edge.
  7. Breakout retest. Price breaks last period's high, comes back to it and holds. Stop back under the upper edge, trail the rest.
  8. Do not chase longs in Extreme bullish, trade the Neutral zone, or take a setup when the timeframes disagree. The scanner already drops setups that hit their target or sit in Extreme bullish.

Edge vs random = the setup's average trade minus what buying on a random day earned over the same number of days on the same stocks. How to trade it

  1. Higher timeframe first. Moxie reads the next timeframe up: weekly Moxie on a daily chart, daily Moxie on an hourly chart. It has to be above zero (green) for a long.
  2. Trampoline potential. Price dips under the 50 SMA while Moxie stays up: the pullback is against the bigger trend. Gray label. Wait.
  3. Trampoline. Price closes back over the 50 SMA with Moxie still up: green square. That close is the trigger.
  4. Your hourly scan looks for the setup forming: weekly Moxie up, and on the hourly chart price under the 50 SMA but still above the 200 SMA (the floor). Watch those for the hourly trampoline.
  5. Stop under the pullback low, or out on a close back under the 50 SMA.
  6. Exit when Moxie turns down (red) on the higher timeframe or crosses below zero.
  7. Moxie cross up (green arrow) is Moxie crossing above zero: momentum just turned up on the higher timeframe.
  8. Trend label is the 8 EMA above the 21 EMA above the 50 SMA. Trampolines with the trend label green are the cleaner ones.

Entry, stop and exit above follow the indicator's logic; your scripts do not define exits. Backtest 2008 to 2026 (Backtests tab): the daily trampoline with a dip low stop beat random buying by about 0.45% per trade; the cross up and the trampoline with the trend label did not.
